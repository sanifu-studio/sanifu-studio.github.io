02

Scope

What we take

  • Product image packs for e-commerce listings, label, colour and proportion checked against source photos
  • Brand character or presenter sheets for one consistent face across campaigns
  • Short explainer video: script and storyboard approved before animation, English voice-over, subtitles
  • Overflow work when your internal team is at capacity

What we don't take

  • Work that requires a live call before the brief is reviewed
  • Rush turnaround under 48 hours
  • Spec or pitch work without a signed brief and, where requested, a signed NDA
  • Live-action shoots. This is AI-assisted visual and video production, not on-location filming

04

White-label terms

  • Your name on it. No Sanifu Studio watermark or credit unless you ask for one.
  • We never contact your client. All communication goes through you.
  • Your retail price is yours. What we invoice you is separate and private between us. Wholesale runs roughly 50–60% of our direct retail price for the same deliverable.
  • Source files included. Your team can make small edits without coming back to us.
  • Turnaround, in writing per project. Image packs typically ship in 3 to 6 business days, short explainer video in 3 to 7.
05

Send a brief

Include these nine points and you will get a written scope, timeline and price within 24 hours.

  1. Agency name
  2. Contact email
  3. Project type: product image pack / character or presenter sheet / explainer video / other
  4. Deliverable spec: quantity, aspect ratios or video length, target platform
  5. Brand guidelines or reference material
  6. Deadline
  7. Budget range
  8. Confirm: this is white-label work, Sanifu Studio will not contact your client directly
  9. NDA needed before further detail? yes / no
